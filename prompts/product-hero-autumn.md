## Prompt

A single handmade speckled ceramic mug in terracotta orange, filled with steaming black coffee, centered on a pale oak table. Soft window light from the left, gentle shadows, a few dried maple leaves placed loosely nearby. Shallow depth of field, warm neutral background, clean negative space on the right for headline text.

## Negative prompt

No text, no logos, no people, no clutter, no harsh flash, no plastic look.

## Notes

Works best when the mug color matches the current seasonal palette. Keep the right third of the frame empty for copy overlay.
