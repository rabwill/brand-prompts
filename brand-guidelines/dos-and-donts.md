## Do

- Use natural, soft, directional light
- Leave negative space for copy
- Show handmade texture: speckles, glaze drips, subtle imperfections
- Keep compositions simple, with three props or fewer
- Favor warm neutral backgrounds

## Don't

- Include people's faces
- Add text, logos, or watermarks inside the image
- Use neon, heavy filters, or oversaturated colors
- Use plastic-looking, glossy 3D-render styles
- Mimic competitor brands or named artists
