## Core palette

- Terracotta: #C8623A (primary accent)
- Oat cream: #F4EBDD (background)
- Charcoal: #2B2B2B (text and shadows)
- Sage green: #8A9A7B (secondary accent)

## Seasonal palettes

- Autumn: terracotta, mustard #D9A441, deep brown #4A3428
- Winter: slate blue #5B7186, frost white #F7F9FB, warm candle glow #F2B66D
- Spring: sage green, blush #E9C6C0, oat cream
- Summer: sand #E3CFA4, sky #9FC5D8, terracotta

## Usage in image prompts

Describe colors in words (for example "terracotta orange") rather than hex codes. Keep one dominant color per image and one accent.
